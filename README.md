ARP Experiences is a relationship-led travel and hospitality company creating exceptional travel and gathering experiences for discerning clients.

Founded by Amber Rose Powers, ARP Experiences brings together over 20 years of experience across fashion, boutique hospitality, experiential design, relationship management, partnerships, and luxury travel.

With a career spanning hotel operations, VIP programming, hospitality leadership, brand partnerships, and travel advising, Amber approaches travel through a hospitality lens — understanding the person, the purpose, the place, and the details that make an experience exceptional.

ARP Experiences connects clients with exceptional hotels, destinations, experiences, and hospitality partners through a highly personal and informed point of view.

We don’t simply arrange travel. We create experiences around it.

THE BRAND IDEA

ARP Experiences is built around a simple belief:

Where you go matters. How you experience it matters more.

Travel is more than a reservation or an itinerary. It is the hotel you remember, the table you gathered around, the people you met, the way a place made you feel, and the details that made everything come together effortlessly.

ARP Experiences brings together travel, hospitality, relationships, and experience design to create travel that feels personal, seamless, and exceptional.

The name ARP gives the brand a distinct identity of its own — one that is rooted in its founder’s expertise while allowing the company to grow beyond a single individual.

Experiences keeps the brand intentionally broad — allowing it to encompass travel, gatherings, retreats, celebrations, hospitality, and the many ways people come together around a place.

FOUNDER POSITIONING

Amber Rose Powers

Founder | ARP Experiences

Amber Rose Powers is a hospitality and travel professional with more than 25 years of experience across fashion, boutique hotels, experiential design, partnerships, relationship management, and luxury travel.

Her career began in fashion, with a degree in Apparel Design and experience spanning design, PR, sales, and brand relationships.

She later moved into real estate development and boutique hospitality, helping launch Detroit Foundation Hotel before serving as its Director of Lifestyle, where she developed experiential programming, managed local and global VIP relationships, and worked alongside a Michelin-starred chef.

She subsequently joined Aparium Hotel Group headquarters in Chicago as Chief Relationship Officer, working across a portfolio of boutique hotels with a focus on relationships, partnerships, culture, and lifestyle-driven initiatives.

In 2022, Amber brought that hospitality perspective into travel advising, developing a deep network of industry relationships and firsthand knowledge of the hotels, destinations, and partners that create exceptional guest experiences.

Today, ARP Experiences brings all of those disciplines together.

Her approach is simple: understand the person, understand the purpose, know the place, and consider every detail.
